# Changelog

## 9.1.4

- Initial release.
- Valkey 9.1.4, thin wrapper on the official `valkey/valkey` image.
- `password`, `maxmemory`, `maxmemory_policy` and `log_level` options; data
  persisted under `/data`.
- Ships an AppArmor profile (`apparmor.txt`).
- `icon.png`/`logo.png` are cropped from Valkey's own official horizontal
  logo (valkey.io/img/valkey-horizontal.svg).
